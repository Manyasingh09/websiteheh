The premise

The world runs on an invisible script: a "canon" romance where a sweet, ordinary heroine wins the prince's heart, while the noble fiancée — you — is humiliated, disinherited, exiled, or executed by the finale. You are the eldest daughter of a declining noble house, betrothed since childhood to the Crown Prince. You know, in your bones, how this story is supposed to end for someone like you. Thornbound is about clawing a different ending out of a narrative that keeps trying to correct itself back to your doom.
